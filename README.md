# Mental-health-wellness-dashboard
A responsive frontend dashboard for tracking mood, stress, sleep, daily habits, and overall mental wellness.

## 1. Project Title

**Mental Health & Wellness Dashboard**

## 2. Introduction

Mental health and emotional well-being are important aspects of a healthy lifestyle. However, people often find it difficult to consistently track their mood, stress, sleep, and daily wellness habits.

The **Mental Health & Wellness Dashboard** is a frontend web application designed to provide users with a simple and interactive platform for monitoring their daily mental wellness. It allows users to record their mood, track stress and sleep, monitor healthy habits, view wellness trends, and access self-care activities through a centralized dashboard.

## 3. Problem Statement

People may experience changes in mood, stress, sleep, and daily habits without being aware of their long-term patterns. A lack of simple and accessible tracking tools can make it difficult to understand these patterns and maintain healthy routines.

Therefore, there is a need for a user-friendly platform that can visually organize wellness-related information and help users become more aware of their daily habits and emotional patterns.

## 4. Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* To create a simple and user-friendly mental wellness dashboard.
* To allow users to track their daily mood and stress levels.
* To monitor sleep and healthy daily habits.
* To visualize wellness data using interactive charts and graphs.
* To provide basic self-care and wellness recommendations.
* To provide a journaling feature for recording daily thoughts.
* To include simple mindfulness and breathing activities.
* To create a responsive interface that works across desktop and mobile devices.

## 5. Proposed Features

### 🧠 Mood Tracking

Users can record their daily mood and view their mood history over time.

### 📊 Wellness Analytics

The dashboard displays important wellness information such as mood, stress, sleep, and habit progress using charts and visual indicators.

### 😴 Sleep Tracking

Users can record their sleeping hours and monitor their sleep patterns.

### 🎯 Daily Habit Tracking

Users can track activities such as:

* Exercise
* Water intake
* Meditation
* Sleep goals
* Other healthy habits

### ✍️ Personal Journal

Users can write and save daily thoughts, feelings, or experiences.

### 🧘 Wellness Activities

The application provides simple mindfulness activities such as breathing exercises and relaxation exercises.

### 🌱 Self-Care Recommendations

Based on the user's tracked information, the interface can display basic wellness suggestions, such as taking a break, exercising, practicing breathing exercises, or improving sleep routines.

### 📈 Wellness Score

A simple wellness score can be generated from tracked factors such as mood, sleep, stress, and daily habits to give users an overall view of their wellness progress.

## 7. System Workflow

The basic workflow of the application is:

**User → Dashboard → Enter Wellness Data → Store Data → Analyze Data → Display Charts & Insights → Wellness Recommendations**

The user enters information such as mood, stress level, sleep hours, and daily habits. The application stores the data locally and presents it through interactive visualizations and progress indicators.

## 8. Expected Outcome

The project is expected to provide a clean and interactive platform where users can easily monitor their mental wellness and daily habits.

The dashboard will help users:

* Understand their mood patterns.
* Monitor stress and sleep.
* Track healthy habits.
* Identify changes in their wellness trends.
* Maintain personal wellness records.
* Practice simple mindfulness activities.

The application is intended as a **wellness tracking and awareness tool and not as a medical diagnostic or treatment system**.

## 10. Conclusion

The **Mental Health & Wellness Dashboard** aims to combine wellness tracking, data visualization, habit monitoring, journaling, and mindfulness activities into a single user-friendly interface. By presenting personal wellness information in an understandable visual format, the project can encourage users to become more aware of their habits and maintain healthier daily routines.
